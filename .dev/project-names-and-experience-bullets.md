# Experience Bullets

Status: editorial draft for review. This document does not change the website or CV.

## Proposed English experience bullets

Keep one bullet per project under its current role. Each prose draft is 300–500 characters; the project page path is excluded from that count. The paths show the proposed new English page locations. When implemented, generate language-aware links rather than copying these paths into every translation.

### brazil-national-water-model

- Description: Assembly and harmonizing streamflow observations, environmental datasets, and geospatial inputs for a national MGB model of Brazil. Develop reproducible preparation and evaluation workflows, assess performance across river basins, and incorporate reservoirs and water uses so the model can support hydrological forecasting and studies of environmental change.

### rs-flood-monitoring-forecasting

- Description: Validate and integrate water-level, streamflow, and geospatial data for flood monitoring in Rio Grande do Sul. Develop basin-scale water-level and streamflow modeling workflows, quality-control procedures, and operational visualizations for warning activities. Explore rating curves to prepare and interpret observations used in flood analysis and forecasting.

### cerrado-topographic-water-conservation

- Description: Develop a terrain-based hydrological-importance indicator to help identify priority areas for water conservation and restoration in the Cerrado. Process high-resolution digital terrain models; combine HAND and topographic wetness measures; define indicator transformations and mapping criteria; and analyze how valley bottoms, wetlands, slopes, and plateaus differ in saturation and infiltration potential.

### brazil-water-availability-geodatabase

- Description: Redesign ANA’s water-availability database by examining its existing tables, relationships, and spatial routines. Design a unified PostgreSQL/PostGIS model, develop processing functions and migration routines for legacy records, and prepare documentation and training materials so staff can query, update, and ingest hydrological and geospatial information consistently.

### river-flow-archive-spatial-database

- Description: Build and maintain a PostgreSQL/PostGIS spatial database for the UK’s National River Flow Archive. Consolidate hydrographic files and station metadata, develop Python routines for ingestion and catchment-attribute calculation, and precompute attributes across the drainage network. Support reproducible updates and data products, including CAMELS-GB, for more than 1,600 stations.

### england-wales-lidar-terrain-models

- Description: Develop workflows that process high-resolution LiDAR into consistent digital terrain and surface models for England and Wales. Merge source elevation data, prepare reusable terrain inputs, and create analysis examples showing how the integrated products can support flood and drought research across different study areas and workflows.

### drone-shrub-mapping-height-estimation

- Description: Develop remote-sensing workflows to map shrubs and estimate their height from drone imagery. Train an Attention U-Net for shrub segmentation, run tiled inference on large RGB images, and convert predictions to shrub outlines. Combine structure-from-motion surfaces, terrain data, and LiDAR reference measurements to derive height predictors and evaluate machine-learning estimates.

### environmental-dataset-curation

- Description: Review submitted environmental research datasets and prepare them for ingestion and publication through the Environmental Information Data Centre. Apply consistent data-management practices throughout preparation and release, resolve data-organization problems that could impede publication or downstream reuse, and help make datasets suitable for other researchers to use.

### brazil-river-network-mgb-integration

- Description: Develop preprocessing methods that connect the MGB hydrological model to Brazil’s official Ottocodified Hydrographic Base. Discretize the vector river network, derive hydrological parameters, and translate hydrographic features into consistent model units and inputs. Evaluate how discretization choices affect national-scale modeling workflows and support reproducible MGB applications.

### brazil-water-accounts-hydrology

- Description: Assemble and analyze national datasets on precipitation, evapotranspiration, soil moisture, and water storage to support Brazil’s environmental-economic water accounts. Quantify spatial and temporal variation in water stocks and fluxes across hydrographic regions, and integrate hydrological estimates with accounting information to describe water availability and change.

### ungauged-streamflow-machine-learning

- Description: Develop machine-learning methods to estimate long-term mean streamflow and Q95 low flow at ungauged locations across Brazil. Assemble observations and environmental predictors, train and compare models, assess performance and uncertainty across hydrological settings, and generate reference-flow estimates for more than 400,000 points on the national river network.

### cerrado-riparian-drainage-mapping

- Description: Develop terrain-based methods to identify drainage networks and riparian zones in the Brazilian Cerrado. Extract streams from digital elevation models, analyze topographic position and variation in drainage density, and combine remote sensing, vegetation indices, and evapotranspiration data to assess riparian vegetation across a large agricultural-expansion region.
